🌿 Project Overview

Our project, "Renewable Energy Solution for Water & Environmental Restoration", aims to provide a sustainable wind-powered water filtration system for areas affected by wildfires. The solution integrates natural filtration media (gravel, sand, lava rock, activated coconut charcoal, cotton), real-time humidity and temperature sensors, a Grove LCD RGB display, and soil health monitoring.

The system is designed to accelerate soil recovery while providing educational and sustainable infrastructure to underserved communities. The modular setup allows for deployment in diverse terrain and post-fire scenarios.

This project was awarded the Terry McManus Outstanding Student Award during the 35th WERC Environmental Design Contest.
