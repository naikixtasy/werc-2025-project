Project Overview

Our system uses wind energy to power a water filtration setup capable of supporting soil recovery after wildfires. Using natural filtration media such as lava rock, sand, coconut charcoal, and cotton, the water is purified and distributed using a modular design. Real-time environmental data is monitored using humidity and moisture sensors, with microcontroller-based automation.
